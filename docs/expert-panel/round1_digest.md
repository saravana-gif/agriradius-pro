## 4. HORTICULTURIST + PLANT PATHOLOGIST/ENTOMOLOGIST
- Open-Meteo has hourly dew point, VPD, ET0, soil moisture; leaf wetness only daily → use hours RH≥90% proxy.
- Rule table: arecanut koleroga (20-23°C, RH 90%+, 15-20 d after onset; spray in dry windows) HIGH for timing; coconut bud rot medium; rhinoceros beetle calendar (Jun-Sep); red palm weevil — scouting only; rugose spiralling whitefly follows deficit monsoon + high Tmax (Pollachi 2016) — advice usually "DON'T spray, conserve Encarsia"; Ganoderma drought-aggravated, scout; arecanut YLD no model; banana Sigatoka (25-28°C, RH>90%, wet) medium; banana Fusarium/bunchy top — not weather, certified TC plants; maize fall armyworm — temperature never limiting in S. India → scouting alert 10-40 DAS from app-detected sowing date, medium-high; paddy blast hourly rule medium; tomato late blight Hutton needs local recalibration. Label every card "conditions favour X — scout before spraying".
- COCONUT DROUGHT LAG = strongest predictive signal: primordium ~32 months before spathe opening + ~12 months nut maturation → ~44 months. CPCRI Kasaragod 1961-84: annual rain doesn't predict yield; number of dry weeks Mar-May in previous 1-2 years explained 59-67% of yield variation (Mathew et al.). Effects: 0-12 mo button shedding, 12-24 fewer female flowers, 24-44 aborted primordia. 2016-17 palms died in Salem. Model: yield_index(t)=a+b1·DryWeeks(t−1)+b2·(t−2)+b3·(t−3), start CPCRI coeffs, refit on CDB district/block production (free annual 2000-01..2025-26), then OneRoot procurement; downgrade for NDMI-irrigated gardens. Output 12/24/36-month supply outlook per village.
- Arecanut lag ~9-12 months (practice estimate); banana: stress at months 3-5 cuts bunch weight 6-8 months later.
- Canopy NDMI/NDVI z-score vs same month (S2 2017+, MODIS 2000+) precomputed monthly; palms stay green after yield is lost → confirmation, not leading signal.
- Field log per harvest: date, nut count, palms, button-shedding score 0-3 → best near-term forecast and lag-model calibration.
- Sources: CDB statistics (no weekly price series found), CPCRI/IIHR/TNAU PDFs for advisory text, NPSS app (no API), Open-Meteo historical/seasonal non-commercial — use NASA POWER instead for history.
- Ranked: (1) coconut volume outlook 12/24/36 mo 5-7d; (2) koleroga/bud-rot spray-window alert 2-3d; (3) harvest+scouting log 3-4d; (4) weekly risk cards FAW/blast/Sigatoka 4-5d; (5) perennial NDMI anomaly 4-6d; (6) drought-linked flags RSW/Ganoderma 1-2d; (7) arecanut/banana outlooks 2-3d.

## 8. AGRI-RISK, INSURANCE, RURAL FINANCE (+ remote sensing)

- Trigger 2 (impact, 3 of 4 types): sown area vs normal (<85% moderate, ≤75% severe); VCI 40-60 / <40 or NDVI/NDWI deviation −20..−30% / <−30%; MAI/PASM 51-75 / ≤50; hydrology indices.
