# Expert panel review — 12 Oct 2026

A simulated panel of nine senior practitioners (agro-meteorology, groundwater/irrigation, soil & agronomy, horticulture & plant protection, spices & tender coconut, commodity trading, field extension, agri-risk & insurance, and a farmer/FPO president) reviewed Ground Intel and debated how to make it real-time, predictive and useful for field decisions at zero extra cost.

- `roadmap.html` — the consolidated plan: the 2026 warning timeline, crop calendar, top 15 improvements, WhatsApp delivery design, build phases, code issues, and verified licence/fact checks. Open it in a browser.
- `panel_brief.md` — the brief every panel member worked from, including the owner's answers (WhatsApp; turmeric, maize, banana, chilli, tender coconut; Karnataka + Tamil Nadu).
- `round1_digest.md` — condensed Round 1 positions of members 1–8.
- `round1_agrometeorologist.md`, `round1_spices_tender_coconut.md` — two full Round 1 reports.

Corrections from the independent fact-check (already applied in `roadmap.html`, not in the round-1 files): the data.gov.in mandi feed has no arrival quantities (use CEDA-Ashoka); the CPCRI coconut finding is ~60% of yield variation from dry-spell length over the preceding two years; Karnataka's drought count reached 177 taluks by early October 2026. Ground Intel is non-commercial NGO work: Earth Engine is free for nonprofits doing noncommercial work (register the project under the NGO), and Open-Meteo's free API covers non-profit apps without subscriptions or ads.
